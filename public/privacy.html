Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Tendr  ·  support@tendr.co.in

Tendr ("we", "our", "us") operates the event planning platform available at tendr.co.in. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using Tendr, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and city. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your Google account name and profile picture from Google.

Event and Planning Data: Information you provide while using planning tools — vendor selections, event dates, guest counts, budgets, checklists, and timelines — is stored to power your personal dashboard.

Usage Data: We collect standard server logs including IP address, device type, browser version, and pages visited to diagnose issues and improve the product.

Payment Information: Payments are processed by Razorpay. Tendr does not store your card or bank details — only the transaction reference number and payment status.

Community Content: Photos, reviews, and posts you share on the Community Wall are stored and displayed to other users of the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

Service Delivery: To create your account, match you with vendors, process bookings, and send event reminders.

Communication: We send transactional messages (booking confirmations, OTPs) via WhatsApp and SMS. We may send occasional product updates — you can opt out at any time.

Product Improvement: Aggregated, anonymised usage data is used to improve features and fix bugs. We do not sell your personal data.

Legal Compliance: We may use or disclose your data when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Tendr, its users, or the public.

3. How We Share Your Information

With Vendors: When you send a booking enquiry, your name, event details, and contact number are shared with the relevant vendor so they can respond to your request.

Service Providers: We use Cloudinary (file storage), Razorpay (payments), and Redis Cloud (temporary session data). These providers process data only as necessary to deliver their service and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, we request the following scopes: your email address, your name, and your profile picture. We use this information solely to create and identify your Tendr account. We do not access any other Google account data. Your use of Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can request account deletion by contacting us at support@tendr.co.in. We will delete your personal data within 30 days of your request, except where retention is required by applicable law.

OTPs and Google sign-in pending tokens are stored in Redis with a short TTL (5–10 minutes) and are automatically and permanently deleted after expiry.

6. Your Rights

Access and Correction: You can view and update your profile information at any time from your Tendr dashboard.

Data Portability: You may request a copy of your personal data by emailing support@tendr.co.in.

Withdrawal of Consent: You may withdraw consent for non-essential communications at any time by contacting us or using the opt-out link in any message we send.

Account Deletion: You may request full deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

7. Security

All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. User passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are never stored in plain text. Authentication tokens (JWTs) are signed with a secret key and expire after 7 days. If you suspect unauthorised access to your account, please contact us immediately at support@tendr.co.in.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to remember your session token, UI preferences (such as saved filters), and tool data (timelines, budgets) you create locally on your device. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. Google OAuth uses its own cookies on Google's domain during the sign-in flow, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

9. Children's Privacy

Tendr is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites or vendor profiles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on the app or sending you a message. The date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last updated. Continued use of Tendr after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
